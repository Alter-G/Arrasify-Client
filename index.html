Getting Started - About

What is Arrasify?

Arrasify is a small chat application on the web, designed to have accounts without necessarily needing anything complicated to get one, like an email or phone number. Originally designed for the web game arras.io, and more specifically the #wpd sandbox server, it has evolved into a vast communication platform for people of any kind, offering easy ways to connect with others, and to socialize.

This project is 100% funded and supported by me and is not monetized or accepting donations. If you would like to help it, you could share it to other people (even people that don't know arras.io)!

Origins

Birth of #wpd

The origins of Arrasify come from around the end of 2023 to the beginning of 2024. In the #wpd sandbox server, at the time, there was two positions in the server-player, and operator (OP). Whilst players had normal permissions like in official gamemodes of arras.io, operators had the ability to build their own minigames, art, and concepts within the arena. This sparked an influx of people wanting to attain such status, but it didn't come without consequence. Because there was only one positon at this time, anyone with the operator permissions could easily promote and demote whoever they wanted. So, the most popular of users at the time decided to create a 'trust' system-a way of quantifying if someone is good enough to become an operator to moderate the server and build stuff at the same time.

This decision made a wall between players who only wanted to unleash creativity and people who needed to keep the server in order, as there were users who oftenly obtained operator by accident and began destroying other people's creations ('griefing'). And eventually it got bad enough to the point where abusers started to impersonate trusted operators in order to tarnish their reputation and to obtain operator for themselves and themselves alone.

Featured YouTubers and Impersonation

At the time, there was also secretly a third role; 'featured youtuber'. Specific users chosen by the developers are gifted access to unreleased tanks, new commands, and more moderation capabilities in sandbox. These users cannot be demoted by people who possess operator, and as such were oftenly contacted to help when abusers invaded the server. One of the most popular arras.io youtubers at the time, also being featured, BIRD (Mushroom), was a prime example of these featured youtubers. They kickstarted the #wpd community back in May of 2023. They are the reason that #wpd exists now.

Whilst they helped out where able, they could not always be available, which is understandable. And as such, people began searching for ways to stop impersonators. One of which was to have a name using a unique font or set of characters that is difficult to replicate. Many people resorted to online font generators, and for a while those worked. But the abusers ended up finding the most common ones and started impersonating again. So people went to more obscure sites, which the abusers did not find. However, there was a script released to the public by an anonymous user that let people copy the names of others without needing to find the exact font generator they used. People were close to having no way of stopping impersonators outside of Discord, which wasn't viable to use as most of the playerbase at the time could not obtain an account. So, that's where Arrasify came in-or, rather, Arras Verify at the time.

Arras Verify is Born

Originally thought by MLG, and developed by MLG, Flowey, and Testing, the site kickstarted on Glitch, a site that lets people host their own web pages for free (there was also a paid tier to boost uptime of the server you hosted). The site did not originally have a chat-just an input box for a token (called 'codes' back then), and a user list of online people. You'd put your own code into the box, and you'd log into your account and join the online users list. This was used as a way to verify if people are actually on the #wpd server, and if they aren't, then whoever is using their name in the server is an impersonator.

This, for a while, worked great. Impersonators got caught more easily, and the site eventually developed a chat so you could communicate outside of the game. And then, one of the biggest abusers and msot infamous at the time, 'TaskMaster', formerly known on youtube as 'wk18', got permanently banned from the game by the developers, meaning they could no longer terrorize or impersonate anyone any longer. And then shortly after, the developers released a sandbox update, adding two new positions to the player hierarchy-'arena conductor' (AC), and 'arena supervisor' (AS). These roles come before operator, which is now renamed to 'arena operator' (AO).

With this new update, abusers were at a new low, and many started to quit because it was harder to grief builds and take control of the server. Leading into 2025, #wpd was lead to a state of peace. Bird eventually quit silently, but the server was still-and is still-popular. And then 2025 came, and Glitch declared they would stop web hosting on July 8th.

Glitch Shutdown, the Replacement, and the Hijack

We were unfortunately not able to create a solution by then, and so Arras Verify became dead for about 6 months (I do not recall the exact time frame). A user called MatriCYS created a Chatzy room alternative to keep communicating, but it did require an email to use. During these 6 months, Testing found a way to port Arras Verify to Vercel, a serverless website hosting service. Paired with PartyKit, a service used to host servers, and Arras Verify has returned.

During February 2026 to 8th of September 2026, nothing unusual happened in the way of Arras Verify. However, Arras.io received a DMCA complaint from diep.io's developer, Mistik, causing one of Arras.io's developers to quit, and the other being radio silent for a year. But somehow #wpd still lived on and kept growing, even with the abscense of featured youtubers not being as active anymore and the countless botters using scripts to crash the servers. But, on September 9th 2026, a vulnerability was found in Arras Verify.

The vulnerability leaked everyone's codes, making the authentication process completely pointless. Impersonators started to steal accounts and even managed to get manager permissions, which allowed them to delete those accounts too. The database got ravaged by griefers and abusers. After an hour, Testing arrived to fix it, and most accounts were recovered over a ~3 day period. But in the background MLG had been working on a new Arras Verify clone for two weeks prior. Deciding on the spot, he released it-Arrasify.

And that is where we are today.

Getting Started - Rules

What Isn't Allowed

Spamming

If multiple users deem you to be spamming, either through messages or commands, stop immediately. If continued, you will receive punishment.

Advertising

Anything promotional that is irrelevent to the conversation currently being held should not be promoted. This rule also oftenly interlaces into spam, and is treated as such.

Language

Most of us speak English, but other languages are allowed. However, refrain from using extreme slurs and hateful words. This rule ties into hate & prejudice.

This rule is lenient, so read the audience before you post.

Media

Refrain from posting graphic imagery, implied innuendos, NSFW content, extreme gore and/or violence, and anything that you think people would likely get uncomfortable with.

Hate & Prejudice

Any kind of prejudice or hate towards anyone, especially catered to specific groups, will usually be warned. If continued, expect exponential punishment.

This rule includes, but is not limited to racism, homophobia, transphobia, anti-religion, and ableism. Make sure your message comes across the right way before posting.

Evasion

Evading punishment, whether it be using a guest account, a vpn, an alternate account, a role, or any other number of methods will result in exponential severe punishment.

VPNs are allowed, but only if used not for the purpose of punishment evasion.

Direct Messages

Don't send anything inappropriate in DMs. Yes, it's an unmonitored area, but if the user is uncomfortable, depending on what is said, you can and will get punished.

Shady People

Anyone already confirmed to be suspicious, proven to broken any aforementioned rules, or done something not appropriate, will be removed without hesitation. It is recommended to not interact with these people.

Any attempts at trying to evade punishments will result in exponentially worse punishment.

Punishments

Block

You will be unable to send direct messages to specific users. The users that block you will also have the option to hide your global messages. This is decided by accounts of Arrasify, and not explicitly chosen by admins.

Mute

You won't be able to send messages for a duration of time. Until that time is over, you cannot communicate to anyone, including DMs. You can be muted in either text, uploads, or both. Mutes are given to people who advertise, spam, or send inappropriate language.

Temp-Ban

You will temporarily be unable to access your account, until the specified duration has passed. You can still see the chat as a guest, but cannot interact with anyone. This is given to people if rules are broken continuously.

Suspension

You will be unable to access your account for an undisclosed amount of time. This is used when admins aren't sure how long you should wait to be forgiven.

Termination

Your account will be completely deleted from the database. Your token will no longer function, and you will need to ask for a new one. This is reserved for extreme rule infraction, often accompanied by shunning or blacklisting.

Shunning

You will not be able to access the site, or any utilities, until the specified duration has passed. All routes and services will be blocked. This is reserved for extreme rule infraction.

Blacklist

You are permanently unable to use our site or our services. This is used if you evade punishments too much, have a bad history of chat infraction, or done other activities that prompt this action.

Moderation Tips for Admins

Moderation from admin-to-admin varies. Because of this, some users may think admins are biased for letting some users get away with breaking the rules. We have no proper standard to follow aside the rules and the appropriate punishments, so if you're accused of being biased, try see what other admins are doing to get a better idea on what should be done.

Inaccurate punishments given to users are fine if they are mistakes, but if it's a continous or conscious choice, you will be demoted. Not everyone is meant to be an admin, and that may not necessarily be your fault, so please don't take it personally. We've seen a few prior admins in old verify tend to have this reaction.

Also, don't feel pressured to be chronically online 24/7. This community might be expanding, but you do not have to sacrifice your own time stressing about the state of this service. It is not a job, and being an admin should be purely based on genuinely wanting to help out, and not for status or obligation.

Getting Started - Admins

This section may contain out-of-date information.

Different Admin Types

Helper

Synopsis

Helpers are the first type of administrator, or moderator. Their purpose is to help lightly moderate the chat, either by muting people who break the basic rules, or directing users to other admins who can help.

Permissions

Mute

Can mute guests and users, for a specific duration of time. The range can be from one minute to four hours.

Underseer

Synopsis

Underseers can do everything helpers can, and also create, edit, and delete accounts. They can promote and demote specific users to and from helper, and can dish out temp-bans.

Permissions

Mute

Mute time range is increased to eight hours.

Temp-Ban

Able to temporarily ban access from accounts of users.

Account Manipulation

Can create, edit, and delete entries from the database of accounts. Can only access the specific codes they themselves added, and as such can also only modify or delete those codes.

Can promote and demote users to and from helpers.

Overseer

Synopsis

Overseers can do everything underseers do, and can modify all accounts without restriction, except for roles higher above itself. Promotion permissions are still the same.

Permissions

Mute

Mute time range is increased to twelve hours.

Slowmode

Able to add a slowmode, in between zero seconds to one minute.

Suspension

Advanced - Commands

Types

First and foremost; selectors. Selectors are the basis of almost every command. Specific commands require different selectors. There are multiple types, but I'll briefly go over the important ones;

Account ID (aID)

Refers to 'account ID'. This is a unique ID given to accounts upon account creation. Only a few commands require it. It is usually reserved for commands that require to edit accounts, even when they aren't online.

Instance ID (iID)

Refers to 'instance ID'. This is a randomly-chosen string of characters which can be used to reference a temporary client. This selector is useful if you want to target guests, but is also used in other commands too.

Database ID (dID)

Refers to 'database ID'. This is the index in the database of the account you possess, and is used to edit permanent information usually found inside the account database. This is most commonly used in the /data command.

Role ID (roleID / rID)

Refers to 'role ID'. This is a string commonly used to find which role does what action. It is used when reading roles on the client and for adding roles to users, as well as adjusting variables custom to that specific role.

Variable ID (varID / vID)

Refers to 'variable ID'. This is used in conjunction with the role ID and is typically used to modify dynamic values of an account that are stored using the roles system.
